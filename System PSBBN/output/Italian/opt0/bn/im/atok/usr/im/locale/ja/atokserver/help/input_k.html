Input character type                          

Select what kind of characters to enter.
Click on the ATOK palette to change the input character type.
If you want to set the mode to always use, change it in the input character type of the input/conversion 1 sheet of ATOK environment settings.

Hiragana
The entered characters will be displayed in full-width hiragana. You can convert to a kanji-kana mixed sentence using the space key, conversion (next candidate) key, or shift + space key.

full-width katakana
The entered characters will be displayed in full-width katakana.
You can convert to a kanji-kana mixed sentence using the space key, conversion (next candidate) key, or shift + space key.

half-width katakana
The entered characters will be displayed in half-width katakana.
You can convert to a kanji-kana mixed sentence using the space key, conversion (next candidate) key, or shift + space key.

Full-width alphanumeric characters
If the Katakana/Hiragana keys are unlocked, full-width alphabets will be displayed; if the Kana keys are locked, full-width Katakana characters will be displayed.
Conversion (Next candidate) You can convert to a sentence with kanji and kana by pressing the key / Shift + space key.

Half-width alphanumeric characters
If the Katakana/Hiragana keys are unlocked, half-width alphabets will be displayed; if the Kana keys are locked, the characters entered will be displayed in half-width Katakana.
Conversion (Next candidate) You can convert to a sentence with kanji and kana by pressing the key / Shift + space key.

Hiragana fixed
It will be entered in hiragana.
Pressing the space key only inputs a blank character and does not convert the characters you type.

Full-width katakana fixed
Entered in full-width katakana.
Pressing the space key only inputs a blank character and does not convert the characters you type.

Half-width katakana fixed
Entered in half-width katakana.
Pressing the space key only inputs a blank character and does not convert the characters you type.

Fixed to full-width alphanumeric characters
Entered in full-width alphabets.
Pressing the space key only inputs a blank character and does not convert the characters you type.

Half-width alphanumeric characters fixed
Entered in half-width alphabets.
Pressing the space key only inputs a blank character and does not convert the characters you type.

Full-width alphabetic characters
Select this to call up a registered word based on its alphabetical reading, or to enter a full-width alphabet while typing.
It cannot be used when inputting kana and kanji.

Alphabetic characters
Select this to call up a registered word based on its alphabetical reading, or to enter a half-width alphabet while typing.
It cannot be used when inputting kana and kanji.
