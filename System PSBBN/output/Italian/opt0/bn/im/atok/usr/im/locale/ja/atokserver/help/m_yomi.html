Correct input errors                           

Even if you make a mistake, there is no need to re-enter all the characters.
Just delete the incorrect part and re-enter it.
The method for correcting input errors varies depending on the status, such as during input, conversion, and after confirmation.

Before conversion

If you notice a mistake before pressing the space bar to convert, correct it as follows:

[Example]
When modifying "Momiji Fari" to "Momijigari"
  1. Enter "Momiji Fari".

  2. Press the ← key to move the cursor behind "Fah".

  3. Press the Backspace key or Ctrl+H (ku) keys to delete "Fah".

  4. Enter "ga".

  5. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  6. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

Converting

Use the Backspace key or Insert key to convert the characters converted using the Space key back to Hiragana and correct any mistakes.

[Example]
When modifying "Momiji Fari" to "Momijigari"
  1. Enter "Momiji Fari" and press the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  2. Press the Backspace or Insert key to change back to Hiragana.

  3. Press the ← key to move the cursor behind "Fah".

  4. Press the Backspace key or Ctrl+H (ku) keys to delete "Fah".

  5. Enter "ga".

  6. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  7. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

After confirmation

If you notice a mistake after confirming the converted characters with the Enter key or Ctrl + M (also) keys, delete the part you want to correct with the Backspace key or Ctrl + H (arrow) keys, and input/convert again.

[Example]
When modifying "Momiji Fari" to "Momijigari"
  1. Press Spacebar to convert and press Enter to confirm.
    You can also convert by pressing Shift + Space key or Convert (Next candidate) key. You can also confirm by pressing Ctrl+M (also).

  2. Press Backspace or Ctrl+H to delete the mistake.

  3. Enter "gari" and press the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  4. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

Move the cursor to the place where you made a typo

If you notice an input error while inputting or converting, press Ctrl + J (ma) keys to move the cursor to the location where the input error is most likely.
If you press Ctrl + J (ma) keys continuously, or if you cannot determine if you have made a typing error, the cursor will move from the beginning of the input reading to the end of the reading.

[Example]
When changing the character string entered as "Cheese Fondue" to "Cheese Fondue"
  1. Enter.

  2. Press Ctrl + J (ma) keys.

  3. Press the Backspace key or Ctrl+H (ku) keys to delete "zu" and enter "dyu".

  4. Press the Spacebar, Shift+Spacebar, or Convert (Next Candidate) key to confirm.

  5. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.
