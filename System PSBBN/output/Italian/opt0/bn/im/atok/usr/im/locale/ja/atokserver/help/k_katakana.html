Convert to katakana or alphabet                                     

Learn how to convert to katakana and alphabet.

[Example]
When inputting "Tomato" and converting it to katakana or alphabet

To enter
When inputting example sentences in Roman letters, use the [T], [O], [M], and [A] keys.
When entering example sentences in kana, use the [to] and [ma] keys.

Make full-width katakana After entering the
characters, press the F7 key or Ctrl+I (to) key.

Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

convert to half-width katakana After entering the
characters, press the F8 key or Ctrl+O (ra) key.

Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

Make full-width alphabet
If you are inputting kana, please press the [Katakana Hiragana] key to enable alphabet input.

After entering the
characters, press the F9 key or Ctrl+P (Se) key.

Press the F9 key or Ctrl+P (Se) key again.

  • Return to hiragana
    While converting, press F6 or Ctrl+U (Na).

    Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

    To return to kana input, press the [Katakana Hiragana] key.

Make it half-width alphabet
If you are inputting kana, please press the [Katakana Hiragana] key to enable alphabet input.

After entering the
characters, press F10 or Ctrl+@.

Press F10 or Ctrl+@ again.

Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

To return to kana input, press the [Katakana Hiragana] key.

Alphabet with initial capital letter
If you are inputting kana, please press the [Katakana Hiragana] key to enable alphabet input.

After entering the
characters, press the F10 key or Ctrl+@ key three times.

Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

To return to kana input, press the [Katakana Hiragana] key.
