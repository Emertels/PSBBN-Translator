Convert                           

Reducing the number of characters to be converted at one time will reduce the number of incorrect phrase breaks, but will require more conversion operations (pressing the space key).
Select the operation method according to your convenience and preference.

Enter the entire text before converting

Enter the entire text and press the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.
To confirm all text at once, press Enter or Ctrl+M. To confirm correctly converted phrases in order from the beginning, press Ctrl + ↓ key or Ctrl + N (mi) key.
Confirming phrases one by one using the Ctrl+↓ key or Ctrl+N (mi) key is called partial confirmation.

[Example]
If you enter "I will go shopping on Sunday"
  1. Enter.

  2. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  3. Press Enter or Ctrl+M to confirm.

Convert each phrase as you enter it

After converting the input characters, if you continue inputting them, the converted phrase will be automatically confirmed.
Confirm that the desired character has been converted, then type and convert the next character, and do this continuously.

[Example]
If you enter "I will go shopping on Sunday"
  1. Enter "Nichiyobiwa".

  2. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  3. Enter "Kimono ni".
    "On Sunday" is confirmed.

  4. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  5. Enter "I'm going out."
    "To shopping" is confirmed.

  6. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  7. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

Select from conversion candidates

After converting by pressing the Space key, Shift + Space key, or Convert (Next candidate) key, you can display other candidates by pressing the Space key, Shift + Space key, or Convert (Next candidate) key.
The window that displays a list of candidates is called a candidate window.

Select and confirm conversion candidates
Place the highlight cursor on the desired word and press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm. Or enter the conversion candidate number.

KeyFunction
Space keyThe inverted cursor moves to the next candidate.
Up keyMoves the inverted cursor to the previous candidate.

Display previous and next candidate groups

KeyFunction
Conversion (next candidate) keyDisplays the next candidate group.
Shift + conversion (next candidate) keyDisplays the previous candidate group.

Changing the clause to be converted

If there is a conversion error in the middle of a sentence, you can correct only the part in the middle of the sentence without confirming the input sentence from the beginning.
Use the ← and → keys to move the target clause to the left and right, and use the Ctrl + ← and → keys to move the target clause to the first and last clause.

[Example]
If you enter "My first voyage this year was a success"
  1. Enter.

  2. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  3. Press the → key to move the featured passage to Publish.

  4. Press the spacebar several times to convert it to "voyage".

  5. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.
