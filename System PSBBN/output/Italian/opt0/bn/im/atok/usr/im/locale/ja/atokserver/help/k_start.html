Introduction                     

ATOK is a Japanese conversion software for creating kanji and kana mixed sentences. The process of creating a kanji-kana mixed sentence can be divided into three stages: input, conversion, and confirmation.

Entering characters from the keyboard and using the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert the entered characters into a kanji-kana mixed sentence is called conversion. If the conversion is as expected, press Enter or Ctrl+M (also). This is called confirm.
