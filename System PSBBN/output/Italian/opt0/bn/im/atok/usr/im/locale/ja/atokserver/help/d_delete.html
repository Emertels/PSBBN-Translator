Delete words from dictionary                           

You can easily delete words from the dictionary, including words that were automatically registered in the dictionary through dictionary learning.
By deleting unnecessary words, you can convert more efficiently.

Easily delete words with keystrokes

Easily delete words with keystrokes

  1. Enter the pronunciation of the word to be deleted.

  2. Press the space bar to convert the word you want to delete.

  3. Press Ctrl+Delete.

  4. Press the Y key.
    The word is removed from the dictionary.
