Input/Convert 　　　　　　

Learn the basic operations of character input.

［Example］
If you enter「君が、パソコンクラブの協力メンバーになって、１年たちました。」

To enter the reading
The keys to use when entering example sentences are as follows.

「きみが、」
Romaji input［Ｋ］［Ｉ］［Ｍ］［Ｉ］［Ｇ］［Ａ］［、］
Kana input［き］［み］［か］［゛］［Shift］+［ね］

「ぱそこんくらぶの」
Romaji input［Ｐ］［Ａ］［Ｓ］［Ｏ］［Ｋ］［Ｏ］［Ｎ］［Ｎ］［Ｋ］［Ｕ］［Ｒ］［Ａ］［Ｂ］［Ｕ］［Ｎ］［Ｏ］
Kana input［は］［゜］［そ］［こ］［ん］［く］［ら］［ふ］［゛］［の］

Enter punctuation
Romaji input［、］キーや［。］キーを押すと、「、」や「。」と入力されます。
Kana input［Shift］キーを押したまま［ね］キーや［る］キーを押すと、「、」や「。」と入力されます。

Enter a small 「ょ」 (sound)
Romaji inputPress［Ｋ］［Ｙ］［Ｏ］［Ｕ］［Ｒ］［Ｙ］［Ｏ］［Ｋ］［Ｕ］to enter「きょうりょく」
If you press［Ｌ］［Ｙ］and［Ｏ], only「ょ」will be input.
Kana inputIf you hold down the [Shift] key and press the［よ］key,「ょ」will be entered.

Enter the「ん」（sound)
ローRomaji inputPress the [N] key twice in succession to input「ん」.
Kana inputPress the［ん］key to input「ん」.

Enter「ー」（long sound)
Romaji inputWhen you press the key,「ー」will be entered.
Kana inputWhen you press the key,「ー」will be entered.

Enter a small「っ」（consonant)
Romaji inputPress［Ｎ］［Ａ］［Ｔ］［Ｔ］［Ｅ］to input「なって」.
If you press［Ｌ］［Ｔ］and［Ｕ］only「っ」will be input.
Kana inputIf you hold down the [Shift] key and press the［つ］key,「っ」will be entered.

Enter「゛」（voiced sound）or「゜」（semi-voiced sound)
Romaji inputPress［Ｂ］and［Ｕ］to enter「ぶ」.
Pressing［Ｐ］and［Ａ］will input「ぱ」.
Kana inputPressing the［゛］or［゜］key to input「゛」or「゜」.

Enter numbers
Romaji inputPress the［１］key to input「１」.
Kana inputAfter pressing［カタカナひらがな］key, press the［ぬ］key to input「１」.
After this, press the［カタカナひらがな］key to enable kana input again.

Convert
Characters displayed on the screen are in an unconverted state immediately after being entered. By pressing the space key, Shift + space key, or the conversion (next candidate) key, it will be converted to a kanji-kana mixed sentence.

convert to kanji
Press the space key, Shift+space key, or the conversion (next candidate) key to convert it to the appropriate kanji.
If you want to convert a kanji to a different kanji than what is displayed in one conversion operation, press the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key again to display different kanji candidates.

convert to katakana
Even if the reading is mixed with katakana, general foreign words will be converted in the same way as kanji.

Determine
Confirm that the desired conversion result is displayed on the screen, then press Enter or Ctrl+M (also). Undefined strings are confirmed. Even if you continue to input readings without pressing the Enter key or Ctrl+M (also) keys after converting using the space key, Shift+space key, or conversion (next candidate) key, it will be confirmed.
