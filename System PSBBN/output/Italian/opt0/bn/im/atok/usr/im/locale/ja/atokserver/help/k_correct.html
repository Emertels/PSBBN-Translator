Before conversion

  1. Enter "You're in that club."

  2. Press the ← key to move the cursor behind the incorrect character.

  3. Press Backspace or Ctrl+H to delete the incorrect character.

  4. Re-enter the correct reading.

  5. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

Top of this page                          

Converting

  1. Enter "You're in the club over there" and use the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  2. Press the Backspace key or Ctrl+H (ku) key to return to the hiragana state before conversion.

  3. Press the ← key to move the cursor behind the incorrect character.

  4. Press Backspace or Ctrl+H to delete the incorrect character.

  5. Re-enter the correct reading.

  6. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

Top of this page                          

After confirmation

  1. Type "You're in the club over there," then press the space bar to convert, then press the Enter key to confirm.
    You can also convert by pressing Shift + Space key or Convert (Next candidate) key. You can also confirm by pressing Ctrl+M (also).

  2. Press the ← key to move the cursor after "Bu" and press the Backspace key or Ctrl+H (ku) key to delete "Aso Shokuku Love".

  3. Enter the correct reading and press the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  4. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.
