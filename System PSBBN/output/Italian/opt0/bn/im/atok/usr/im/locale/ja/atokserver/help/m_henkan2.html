Sort conversion candidates

When converting a name that has many conversion candidates, it is time-consuming to search for the desired conversion candidate.
In such cases, by sorting by the first or last letter, candidates that match the first or last letter will be displayed together, making it easier to find the candidate.
Click [Sort first character] to sort by first character, Click [Sort by last character] to sort by the last character.
Pressing Ctrl+PageDown key or Ctrl+PageUp key has the same function.

[Example]
When entering "Yoshihiro"
  1. Enter "Yoshihiro".

  2. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.
    Press the spacebar a few times and a suggestion window will appear.

  3. Press the space key to move to "Yoshihiro" in the candidate window.

  4. Click [Sort first character].
    The suggestions are sorted and words with the same first letter appear together.

  5. Highlight "Yoshihiro" in the candidate window and press the Enter key or Ctrl + M (also) key.

Properly convert even if input from particles

Even if you enter the phrases into small sections, they can be converted correctly.
If you convert and confirm the sentence "Alps no Yama" as "Alps" and then input and convert it as "Noyama", it will not become "Noyama" but will be correctly converted as "Noyama".

  1. Click [Preferences] on the ATOK palette.

  2. Select the input/conversion 2 sheet.

  3. Select Yes for the particle recovery priority.

  4. Click OK.

Convert spoken words preferentially

Colloquial language, such as spoken words, can be converted preferentially.
You can easily convert colloquial expressions such as ``I did it'', ``It's big'', and ``Let's say it'', making it convenient for creating e-mails and computer communications.

  1. Click [Preferences] on the ATOK palette.

  2. Select the input/conversion 1 sheet.

  3. Select to use colloquial style first.

  4. Click OK.

Prioritize conversion of names and place names

When there are multiple conversion candidates such as a full name and a place name with the same reading, you can convert either the full name or the place name preferentially.
If you want to create a document that includes a mixture of names and place names, select Proper nouns first.

  1. Click [Preferences] on the ATOK palette.

  2. Select the input/conversion 1 sheet.

  3. Select compound word in Kanji conversion mode.

  4. Select Name priority in Compound word conversion details.
    If you want to give priority to the name, select Name priority, and if you want to give priority to place name, select Place name priority.

  5. Click OK.

Convert unregistered words to hiragana one character at a time from the end

When converting to katakana using the F7 key or Ctrl+I key, if you press the key consecutively, you can convert each character from the end of the word to hiragana.
Once confirmed, the katakana part will be learned as a word in the dictionary, and from now on you will be able to convert it just by pressing the space key.

[Example]
If you enter "visit Aix-les-Bains"
  1. Enter.
  2. Press F7 or Ctrl+I.
  3. Press F7 or Ctrl+I again.
  4. Type "Ask".
  5. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.
  6. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

Determine the particle at the beginning of the clause

While inputting or changing the way clauses are separated, you can confirm only the first character of the unconfirmed part by pressing Shift + ↓ key.
This is useful when determining the particle at the beginning of a clause.
If the phrase is highlighted during conversion, use the Backspace key or Insert key to cancel the conversion before executing.

[Example]
When converting "The road to the usual camp" to "The road to the usual cram school"
  1. Enter.

  2. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  3. Press Ctrl+↓ or Ctrl+N (Mi) to confirm "Always".

  4. Press the Backspace key to cancel the conversion.

  5. Press Shift + ↓ key to confirm "no".

  6. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  7. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.

Confirm only the first or last character of the converted phrase

You can confirm only the first or last character of the clause being converted.
Confirm the first character with the Page Down key and the last character with the Page Up key.
You can easily enter kanji that are difficult to convert, such as kanji with unusual readings or names.

[Example]
When entering "Nao"
  1. Enter "nara".

  2. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  3. Press the PageDown key to confirm "NA".

  4. Enter "center".

  5. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  6. Press the PageUp key to confirm "center".
