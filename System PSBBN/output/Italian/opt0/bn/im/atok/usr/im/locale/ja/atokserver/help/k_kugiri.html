Re-separate clauses and convert them to kanji                           

ATOK analyzes the context and derives the optimal kanji-kana mixture sentence.
By performing conversion at meaningful breaks in the text, more accurate conversion results can be obtained.
If the conversion still doesn't work, try the following:

[Example]
When you enter "Usutō no rose wo" and it is converted to "Usutō no rose wo", you can re-separate the clauses and read "Usutō no rose wo".

Re-separate clauses

  1. Enter "Usume no Rose" and press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  2. Press the ← key once to change the division between "Usual" and "Usual".

  3. Press the space key, Shift+space key, or convert (next candidate) key to convert.

  4. Press Enter or Ctrl+M (also) to confirm.
