Check and point out omissions in expressions and uniformity of writing style                           

Equipped with JUST MEDDLER2, which checks sentences at the time of input/conversion and supports accurate sentence expression.
It helps you create more beautiful Japanese expressions by pointing out easily confused expressions and errors in kana usage during the input stage.
It is also possible to point out model-dependent characters that should be noted when communicating with e-mails or computers.

About proofreading support function

Pointing out continuous particles
In the input sentence, points out where the case particles "no", "de", "ga", and "wo" are consecutive are pointed out during conversion.
[Example]
      We'll meet at the park behind my house.
      Make a cake using strawberries.

Pointed out blank expression
When converting by pressing the space key, Shift + space key, or convert (next candidate) key, the text you entered will be checked for empty expressions such as "you can see" and any inappropriate parts will be pointed out.
You can also automatically correct the expression.

Unification of writing style
In order to unify the style of the sentences into "desu•masu style" and "da•desaru style", we will point out the parts that are inconsistent.

Identification of modification relationship
Points out places where the dependence of modifiers (adjectives, adjectives, etc.) may be ambiguous.
[Example]
      Girl with big black eyes
      New Tokushima friend's house

Pointing out difficult-to-understand negative expressions
I will point out places where there are double negatives in the sentences that are difficult to understand.
[Example]
      There's nothing I haven't thought about
      It's not that I don't know

Notice of misuse
It points out idioms and proverbs that are easily confused and displays the correct usage.
[Example]
      get the target → hit the target, get the hit

Pointed out errors in kana usage
Based on the ``Modern Kana Usage'' announced by the Cabinet in 1986, we check for errors in kana usage that are easy to make mistakes, and display the correct kana usage.

Notice of trademark name
We will point out trademarks and product names that are easily confused with common nouns.
If there is a common name, it will be displayed as a possible replacement.

Notice of model-dependent characters
We will point out machine-dependent characters such as external characters and rounded numbers that do not meet the JIS standard and may become invisible or garbled depending on the computer being used.

Use the proofreading support function

Select the function you want to use.

  1. Click [Preferences] on the ATOK palette.

  2. Select the input/conversion 1 sheet.

  3. Turn on Proofing Assistance and click Settings.
    The calibration support settings dialog box will be displayed.

  4. Set each item as necessary.

  5. Click OK.
    Return to input/conversion 1 sheet.

  6. Click OK again.
