Convert to Kanji                           

Learn how to convert to kanji.

[Example]
If you enter "My name is Toshio Kimura"

Confirm from front to back
If the characters you input cannot be converted into the desired kanji at once, they will be confirmed in order from the beginning.
Press Ctrl+↓ key or Ctrl+N (mi) key.

Press Ctrl+↓ or Ctrl+N (mi) again.

Press Ctrl+↓ or Ctrl+N (mi) again.

Change to "Toshio"
If there are many homonyms, such as a person's name, you can choose different kanji.
Once converted, press the spacebar again.

Press the space bar repeatedly until the desired kanji appears.

You can also select the desired kanji by pressing the number (3 in this case) displayed in the list.
