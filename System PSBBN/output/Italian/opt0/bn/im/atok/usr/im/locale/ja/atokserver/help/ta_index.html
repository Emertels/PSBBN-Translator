Word registration                           

Register words in the dictionary.
If you register words that are difficult to convert or frequently used symbols in the dictionary, they will be added to the conversion candidates and you can convert them using the space key.

■ Explanation of setting items

  1. Words
    Enter the word to be registered in the dictionary using up to 64 characters, regardless of whether it is full-width or half-width.
    You can register kanji, hiragana, katakana, alphabets, numbers, and symbols.

  2. Reading
    Enter the reading of the word within 32 characters, regardless of full-width or half-width.
    You can use full-width hiragana, full-width and half-width katakana, alphabets, numbers, and symbols (' ' - + * / _ # $ % & = @ : ; • ' ^ ~ ' < > '¥). Spaces and kanji are not allowed.
    You cannot use -' ' at the beginning of a reading.

  3. Part of speech
    Set the appropriate part of speech to improve conversion accuracy.

  4. Dictionary
    Set the dictionary to register words.

  5. [Continuous registration off/on]
    Switch whether to register words consecutively or not.
    When clicked, [OK] switches to [Register] and [Cancel] switches to [Close], allowing you to register words continuously without closing the word registration dialog box.
